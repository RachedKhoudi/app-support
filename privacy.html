Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Misplaced is a puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: your game stays on your device and we never see it. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and those ads collect some information, as described below.

What the game stores

To remember where you are, Misplaced saves a few things on your device only:

  • your progress: the levels you solved, their keys and your best attempts
  • your buttons, your streak and the days you played
  • your settings, such as light or dark appearance
  • whether you turned on the streak reminder

This information never leaves your device. We have no account system, no server holding your data and no way to see it. If your device backs up apps (for example, iCloud or Google backup), the saved game can be part of that backup, under your backup provider's terms.

Notifications

If you accept the grandmother's offer, the game asks your permission to send notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your device by the system. No push service is used and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device's settings.

Ads

Misplaced is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then between levels, and short ads you choose to watch for buttons. To show and measure these ads, Google's ad software on your device collects information such as your device's advertising identifier, its type and system version, its IP address and approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses it under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Personalised ads. On iPhone and iPad, the game asks whether ads may track you across other apps and websites. If you say no, Google gets no advertising identifier and the ads you see are not based on your activity elsewhere. On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • Your consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before ads use your information, and you can change your answer at any time with Privacy choices in the game's settings.
  • US state privacy rights. If you live in a US state whose law gives you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising (such as California, Virginia or Colorado), you can opt out with Privacy choices in the game's settings.
  • The game itself sends nothing to us: we only see totals that AdMob reports, such as how many ads were shown.

What the game does not do

  • It doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos or any other personal information.
  • It doesn't use analytics or crash reporting services.
  • Apart from the ads described above, it doesn't share any data, and it never sells it.

Children

Misplaced is not directed at children under 13. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone; the ads work as described above.

Deleting your data

Deleting the game removes everything it saved. You can also start over at any time with Reset progress in the game's settings.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what it does with information, for example by comparing your results with other players online, this policy will be updated first, and the new version will say what changed. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

What changed on November 24, 2026: the game now shows ads from Google AdMob (see Ads).
